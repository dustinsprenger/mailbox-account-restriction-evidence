# Apple terms and the Runbox–Mistral–CERN connection: facts and open questions

Added 26 October 2026. These records concern provider policies and a shared AI supplier. No reviewed source establishes a direct Runbox–CERN integration, CERN access to Runbox email, or a connection to the mailbox.org sending restriction.

## Apple: terms changed, and the changes matter for preserving records

The [Apple iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/us-en/terms.html), revised 14 September 2026, were compared with the [29 August archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20260829175847/https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/us-en/terms.html), which contains the 15 September 2025 version.

| Section | Earlier text versus newer text |
|---|---|
| I.C, storage limits | The newer version expressly permits deletion of stored data above the storage limit after 30 days’ notice. That specific sentence is absent from the earlier section. |
| I.E, agreement changes | The newer version expressly warns that declining agreement updates can result in loss of service access. |
| II.G.6, iCloud+ | The newer version adds Apple Intelligence, including personal-context features, app actions, screen awareness and possible usage limits. |

Other suspension and deletion powers already existed. These changes do not establish that Apple newly claimed ownership of research, enabled unrestricted AI training on all files, or deleted any of my evidence. The practical unanswered question is how the newer provisions are implemented: which over-quota data is selected, what notice reaches the account holder, and what export opportunity remains before access or data is lost.

## Runbox: a specific logging change and a disclosure mismatch

The [19 May 2026 archived Boxy notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20260519040313/https://boxy.runbox.com/boxy-privacy.html) said:

> Your messages to Boxy are never logged or retained by Runbox.

It retained chatbot replies for 30 days. The [current Boxy notice](https://boxy.runbox.com/boxy-privacy.html), captured on 25 October and checked again on 26 October, describes sanitized user messages and chatbot replies retained for ten days. It says Mistral screens chatbot messages and performs daily automated review of sanitized logs; staff can also request individual translations.

The separate [general AI policy](https://runbox.com/about/runbox-generative-ai-policy/), also checked on 26 October, describes ten-day logging of Boxy's responses without equally explaining user-message logging in that passage. It says Boxy cannot access accounts or emails. The detailed notice says providers do not train their models on these messages.

This supports a question about disclosure consistency and user notice. It does not demonstrate mailbox-wide AI access. The exact first-change date remains unresolved: May predates the earlier investigation's four-month review window. No finding here establishes that any particular private email entered Boxy or Mistral.

## The CERN connection is through Mistral

[CERN announced on 15 October 2026](https://home.cern/cern-and-mistral-join-forces-to-accelerate-ai-for-science/) that CERN and Mistral signed a statement of intent for a long-term AI-for-science partnership. Its initial aim is a physics foundation model trained on simulated particle collisions.

The supported relationship is:

```text
Runbox's Boxy chatbot ── disclosed AI processing supplier ──> Mistral AI
CERN                 ── separate partnership announcement ──> Mistral AI
```

A common supplier is a documented institutional connection. It is not a demonstrated data-sharing channel between the two customers. CERN's announcement does not describe Runbox data, email filtering, moderation of independent research, or mailbox.org account decisions.

## What readers can reasonably ask

Runbox should reconcile its logging notices and identify when user-message logging began, what notice users received, and how redaction, retention and provider deletion are verified. Apple can clarify the operational safeguards around its newer storage and agreement provisions. The CERN announcement documents a separate scientific partnership with a supplier Runbox uses.

Those are specific, testable questions. The evidence does not currently connect these policies or relationships to the sending ban documented in the main report. Chronological proximity and overlapping suppliers alone cannot establish targeting or coordination.
