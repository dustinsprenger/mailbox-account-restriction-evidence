PUBLICATION METHOD — 26 October 2026

Original files remain unchanged in the private source archive. SHA-256 hashes of original EML files, decoded attachments and published derivatives are recorded. A hash identifies bytes; it is not independent authentication of the original sender, server state, capture time or delivery.

The subscription PNG is an exact byte-for-byte copy. The earlier forum text is an exact copy of the locally preserved published-text record. The later screenshot is an unedited browser capture. The later text is rendered document.body.innerText plus capture time and source URL; it is not a raw page archive and does not include hidden attributes or session-linked URLs.

Public EML files are newly constructed derivatives. From/To fields use example.invalid placeholders. Original Date, Subject and Message-ID identifiers are retained and labeled. All transport, Received, authentication, DKIM, MIME attachment and other original headers are omitted. Body text is selected from the original plain-text part or extracted from HTML with markup removed. Paragraph whitespace may be normalized. Private email addresses become domain-only redaction markers; URL query strings/fragments, IPv4 addresses and the author's street/telephone are removed when encountered. Quoted third-party statements are preserved as source content, not endorsed findings.

Attachments are not embedded in public EMLs. Their original filenames, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are listed in evidence-ledger.json. The research papers are linked through their OSF records rather than duplicated here. Original EMLs and attachments are retained locally for any appropriately scoped verification.

SHA256SUMS.txt covers every other public file, not itself. The original-text hash and recipient-only redaction are documented separately in rejection-exact-redacted.txt. No substantive email body has been rewritten to strengthen the allegations. Any corrections should be committed as visible revisions rather than silently replacing the evidence.
